# Code of conduct

**Keep contributions respectful, relevant and free of private information.**

Be respectful, assume good intent and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and sharing other people's private information are not accepted.

The maintainer may remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. To report conduct that cannot be discussed publicly, open an issue asking for a private contact route without naming people or describing the incident. Wait for that route before sharing details. Security problems follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
